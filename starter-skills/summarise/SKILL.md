# Summarise

Use this skill whenever the user asks for a summary, a recap, a TL;DR or "what is this
about" of a longer text.

Work in three passes:

1. **Mark** — go through the text once and pick out the sentences that carry the meaning.
2. **Write** — write the summary in the length the user asked for: one sentence, one
   paragraph, or a short list of bullet points.
3. **Check** — go over the summary once more against the original: every point in it has
   to be in the text, and nothing important may be missing.

Rules:

- Keep the wording of the source where it is short and clear.
- No new facts, no opinions, no filler ("the text talks about …").
- The most important point comes first.
- When the user did not say how long, give three to five bullet points.
- Say what is missing instead of pretending the text is complete.

Before you answer, read `checklist.md` in this skill and check your summary against it.
